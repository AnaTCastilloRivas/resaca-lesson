Image: KRGV Channel 5 News, “Resaca cleanup targets invasive water hyacinths,” June 2026

In June 2026, crews spent weeks clearing water hyacinth from San Benito’s resaca, in “The Resaca City.” By October, the plants had covered the water again.

Who depends on this water? ¿Quiénes dependen de esta agua?

The resaca is San Benito’s main water source and a main irrigation canal. When hyacinth clogs it, families and farmers lose water they need.

Understanding Our Mathematical Model

Entendiendo nuestro modelo matemático

Discussion · Discusión

How could we write one mathematical rule to represent this pattern?¿Cómo podríamos escribir una sola regla matemática para representar este patrón?

f(x) = a·bx

An exponential function changes by the same multiplicative factor over equal intervals.Una función exponencial cambia por el mismo factor multiplicativo en intervalos iguales.

Our water hyacinth modelP(x) = 4(2)x
a = 4 initial plant populationpoblación inicial b = 2 the population doubles every two weeksse duplica cada dos semanas x number of two-week periodsnúmero de periodos de dos semanas P(x) predicted number of plantsnúmero de plantas predicho
Cognates · Cognados: exponential = exponencial · factor = factor · initial value = valor inicial · asymptote = asíntota

Open-ended question on Padlet · Pregunta abierta

If our model were P(x) = 8(2)x, what would change in the real-world situation? Explain.Si nuestro modelo fuera P(x) = 8(2)x, ¿qué cambiaría en la situación real? Explica.

The starting population would change from 4 to 8 plants, but the population would still double every two weeks.
Back to our graph · Nuestra gráfica

What I would do · Lo que haría

  1. Invite students to explain the rules they discovered in Explore, in English, Spanish, or both.
  2. Connect their own words to f(x) = abx and the formal vocabulary.
  3. Return to the graph and identify the y-intercept (0, 4): the plants at Week 0.
  4. Extend to all real x: horizontal asymptote y = 0. Our real-world model uses x ≥ 0.
  5. Ask: Why does repeated multiplication curve instead of making a straight line?

Student investigation · Investigación

  1. What happens to the graph when a increases from 4 to 8? ¿Qué pasa cuando a aumenta de 4 a 8?
  2. What happens when b decreases from 2 to 1.5? ¿Y cuando b baja de 2 a 1.5?

Evaluate
5 min

Exit Ticket: Can You Model the Change?

Boleto de salida: ¿Puedes modelar el cambio?

1

Interpret · Interpreta

A simplified plant population model is P(x) = 16(1.5)x, where x represents two-week periods. a. What does 16 represent? b. What does 1.5 represent? c. Is this growth or decay?¿Qué representa 16? ¿Y 1.5? ¿Es crecimiento o decaimiento?

a. Initial population: 16 plants. b. Growth factor 1.5: a 50% increase every two weeks. c. Exponential growth (b > 1).
2

Apply · Aplica

A different section of the waterway begins with 80 plants. After a hypothetical control treatment, the population decreases by 25% every two weeks. a. Write an exponential function. b. Predict the population after four weeks.Otra sección empieza con 80 plantas y disminuye 25% cada dos semanas. Escribe la función y predice la población después de cuatro semanas.

a. P(x) = 80(0.75)x. b. Four weeks = 2 periods: P(2) = 80(0.75)2 = 45 plants.
3

Reason · Razona

Why would it be unreasonable to assume the plants will keep growing exponentially forever? Explain one limitation of our model.¿Por qué no es razonable suponer que las plantas crecerán exponencialmente para siempre? Explica una limitación de nuestro modelo.

Limited space and nutrients, environmental changes (drought, cold), cleanup crews, or biological control can stop unlimited growth.
